### [7:00 – 7:40] Slide 14: Recommendation 1: Spatial Navigation & Container Transforms
* **Visual on Screen:** Slide 14 (Recommendation 1: Container Transforms & Slowed Transitions)
* **Spoken Dialogue:**
  > "Our qualitative observations revealed three key interaction friction points that form our three actionable recommendations.
  > 
  > First, on Slide 14, participants noted that drilling into phase details or deliverable cards felt instantaneous and jarring via default platform page pushes. Grounded in Wang and Yu (2024) and Nielsen Heuristic #4, we recommend upgrading to Material 3 Container Transforms and slowing transition durations to 450 to 600 milliseconds. This morphs the card into the full screen, preserving spatial orientation. This recommendation directly fulfills the Assignment 4 criteria for slowed transitions and container transforms."

---

### [7:40 – 8:20] Slide 15: Recommendation 2: Direct Manipulation Gestures
* **Visual on Screen:** Slide 15 (Recommendation 2: Direct Manipulation Gestures)
* **Spoken Dialogue:**
  > "Second, on Slide 15, during Task 2, Participant 2 instinctively double-tapped the mockup image to zoom in, while Participant 1 tried to swipe notification cards to dismiss them. Grounded in Shneiderman's direct manipulation principles and Jakob et al. (2022), we recommend implementing native touch gestures: horizontal swipe-to-dismiss on notifications using Dismissible widgets, and double-tap pinch-to-zoom on wireframes using InteractiveViewer. This recommendation directly fulfills the Assignment 4 criteria for interactive gestures."

---

### [8:20 – 9:00] Slide 16: Recommendation 3: Simulated Backend & Persistence
* **Visual on Screen:** Slide 16 (Recommendation 3: Simulated Backend Repository & Persistence)
* **Spoken Dialogue:**
  > "Third, on Slide 16, during Task 3, participants worried that an accidental browser refresh would wipe out signed certificates, and instant state mutations lacked network feedback. In accordance with Nielsen Heuristic #1 and Li et al. (2025), we recommend replacing static lists with an asynchronous simulated backend repository with realistic network latency, visible sync indicators, and local storage persistence. Client comments, approval hashes, and privacy switches will permanently survive device reloads, directly fulfilling the Assignment 4 criteria to eliminate mock hardcoding and implement persistence."
