### Part A: Participant Information Sheet

#### 1. Purpose of the Study
You are invited to participate in a human-computer interaction (HCI) usability evaluation of **ElegantLink**, a mobile client governance platform developed for Australian custom software consultancy Elegant Media. The purpose of this study is to evaluate the usability, learnability, efficiency, and error prevention of the mobile application against pre-defined usability engineering benchmarks as part of the student's COIT20270 coursework at CQUniversity Australia.

#### 2. Scope, Tasks, and Duration
* **What you will be asked to do:** You will be asked to interact with a mobile prototype running on an interactive device or computer screen. You will attempt **three (3) realistic task scenarios**:
  1. *Task 1 (Learnability):* Review project health and identify the active development phase upon cold app launch.
  2. *Task 2 (Efficiency):* Locate a design wireframe deliverable, review comments from the agency, and post contextual feedback.
  3. *Task 3 (Error Prevention):* Review milestone governance criteria, verify commercial liability commitments, and execute the approval process.
* **Duration:** The evaluation session will take approximately **fifteen to twenty (15–20) minutes**.

#### 3. Participant Rights & Ethical Safeguards (CQUniversity Ethical Protocol)
In compliance with CQUniversity research evaluation requirements and the Australian Privacy Act 1988, your participation is safeguarded by the following six fundamental rights:

1. **Voluntary Participation:** Your participation is completely voluntary. You are under no obligation to participate. Choosing not to participate will involve no penalty or loss of benefit to which you are entitled.
2. **Privacy, Anonymity, and Video Recording:**
   * Your interaction during the session (screen interaction and audio/video of the session) will be recorded to capture task completion times, navigation paths, and user feedback.
   * Your personal identity will remain confidential. In all documentation, you will be identified solely by an anonymous participant code (e.g., Participant 1, Participant 2, Participant 3).
   * **Storage and Non-Publication:** Video recordings will **NOT** be broadcast, published publicly, or uploaded to social media. Recordings are stored exclusively within secure CQUniversity-authorized cloud infrastructure (OneDrive) and are accessible solely to CQUniversity academic marking staff for assessment verification.
3. **Right to Withdraw:** You have the unconditional right to discontinue participation at any time without giving a reason and without prejudice or penalty. If you withdraw, all recorded data and video relating to your session will be permanently deleted upon request.
4. **Seeking Information:** You are encouraged to ask any questions regarding the evaluation, the mobile application, or the evaluation procedures at any stage. You may contact the student investigator at any time.
5. **Complaints Procedure:** If you have any concerns or complaints regarding the ethical conduct of this evaluation, you may contact the CQUniversity Unit Coordinator directly:
   * **Name:** Jamie Shield
   * **Role:** Unit Coordinator, COIT20270
   * **Email:** `j.shield@cqu.edu.au`
   * **Institution:** Central Queensland University Australia
6. **Access to Results:** You are entitled to receive a summary copy of the final usability evaluation report, tabulated results, and design recommendations. Please indicate on the consent form if you wish to receive these findings.
